01 · Product

Ask in plain words. GROOT works out the rest.

You don't need to know which files, spreadsheets or systems hold the answer. Say what you want to know or decide, and GROOT plans the work.

Quick answer “What were Q2 sales in Maharashtra?”

A straight answer from your own data.

Find the cause “Why did sales decline?”

Likely reasons, with the evidence behind each one.

Make a decision “Should we switch suppliers?”

What you know, what's missing, and what to check next.

Build a list “Find 30 manufacturers matching these constraints.”

A clean list, ready to use.

02 · Architecture

How a question becomes a decision

01
Listen

Understands a typed question, or what people say in a meeting.

02
Plan

Turns the request into a clear task: what to find, by when, and how sure it needs to be.

03
Look around

Checks the files, systems and outside sources your team has allowed.

04
Map the decision

Lays out the options, what's known, what's missing and what's being assumed.

05
Fetch what's missing

Collects only the information the task needs. Nothing extra.

06
Keep track

Holds the decision open until the missing pieces are filled in.

07
Learn

Compares each decision with what happened next, so the next one goes better.

04 · Roadmap

Built in six steps.

Each step proves one piece works before the next one starts.

Step 1Question to task

Turn a typed question into a clear task GROOT can run every time.

Step 2Connect data

Hook up files, one internal system and one approved outside source.

Step 3Map decisions

Show what's known, missing and assumed, not just a single answer.

Step 4Meeting mode

Let a spoken discussion start or update the same decision.

Step 5Decision memory

Save each decision and what happened afterwards.

Step 6Learning loop

Use past results to handle similar decisions better next time.

Most assistants wait for a question. GROOT also hears the one your team hasn't asked yet.
